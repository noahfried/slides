♠ Classroom Blackjack

Side bets

Turn on “Side bets” at the top, then choose which ones to offer. Changes take effect at the next betting round.

Your first two cards are a pair. Same suit 25:1, same colour 12:1, other 6:1.

Your two cards plus the dealer’s face-up card. Suited trips 100:1, straight flush 40:1, trips 30:1, straight 10:1, flush 5:1.

By cards in the dealer’s busted hand. 3 cards 1:1, 4 cards 2:1, 5 cards 9:1, 6 cards 50:1, 7 cards 100:1, 8+ cards 250:1.

House rules

Six decks. Blackjack pays 3:2. Dealer stands on all 17s. Double on any two cards. Split up to four hands; split aces get one card each. No insurance.
Keys: H hit, S stand, D double, P split, Enter deal / next.
