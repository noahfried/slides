# New York

How to edit this file: each `###` is a slide. `NOTE:` lines are for Noah and won't appear on slides. `Source:` lines become the tiny source text. Delete a slide to drop it, and rewrite anything you like. New `Claude:` lines are fine too; I'll follow them when I build.

NOTE: Shape of the lesson. (1) A "guess the price" opener with two homes; (2) "Why New York?" and the word cloud; (3) Density; (4) Possibility, with a quick "Can you find it in New York?" yes/no game; (5) back to the homes: the same budget in all five boroughs, with a vote; (6) a zoom into Manhattan, one quick slide per neighborhood; (7) a closing activity where each student picks where to live. Students guess or vote about every five minutes.

NOTE: The budget is **$1.2 million** throughout. That is almost exactly the median Manhattan sale (about $1.25 million in Q3 2026) and about three times the price of a typical American home.

NOTE: Slides follow the 5x5 rule. Pictures do most of the work.
